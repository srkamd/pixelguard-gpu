Privacy Policy for PixelGuard: Document Inspector

Effective Date: October 16, 2026

Developer: Shahrukh Ahmad (github.com/srkamd)

Key Privacy Commitment: PixelGuard operates 100% on-device. We do NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any of your documents, receipts, images, or personal data. Everything is processed locally on your smartphone.

1. Information Collection and Use

PixelGuard is built with privacy-first architecture:

  • Images and Documents: When you select or capture an image for inspection, the analysis runs entirely using an on-device local machine learning model. No images are ever uploaded to an external server or cloud storage.
  • Personal Information: We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, email address, phone number, financial details, or device identifiers.
  • Offline Functionality: The application functions completely without an active internet connection.

2. Device Permissions Requested

To provide core inspection services, the app requests the following minimum permissions:

  • Camera (Optional): Used solely to allow you to take a direct photo of a document for inspection within the app. Images captured are processed in memory and never saved to any external server.
  • Photo Picker / Media Access: Used solely to allow you to select a document from your gallery for inspection.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support the free version of this application, we may display third-party advertisements via Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect pseudonymous advertising identifiers subject to their own privacy policies.

You can review Google's Privacy Policy here: Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Data Retention and Security

Because no user data is collected or transmitted to external servers, we hold no user data on our infrastructure. Your data stays securely on your physical device at all times.

5. Disclaimer

PixelGuard provides algorithmic image noise, steganography, and compression residual inspection for informational and digital hygiene purposes. It does not replace official banking verification or legal forensic certification.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via GitHub: github.com/srkamd/pixelguard-gpu.
